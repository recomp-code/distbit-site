Questions we hear often

Not covered here? Write to our team.

Why use stablecoins instead of a bank wire?+

Wires depend on correspondent banks, cut-off times and business days. Stablecoins settle in seconds at any hour, and every movement leaves a verifiable record. Distbit converts back to pesos or dollars at the edges, so your customers never have to touch crypto.

Why would I hold a peso stablecoin?+

If your costs and revenue are in pesos, converting everything to dollars adds FX exposure. MXN stablecoins such as MXNB, MXNT and MMXN let you settle on-chain and stay in pesos, then cash out to SPEI whenever you need.

Do I need to replace my current systems?+

No. Distbit sits beside your ledger, core banking or payments provider. Pick the pieces you need, such as the Peso Gateway or Embedded Vaults, and connect them through the API.

How do you handle compliance?+

We identify every customer and business, screen against sanctions lists and monitor transactions under Mexico's anti-money-laundering framework. Details are in our Terms of Service below.
